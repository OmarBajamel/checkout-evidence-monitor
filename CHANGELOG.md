On 2026-09-24 the documented local verification completed: 73 listed pytest cases, 20 fresh installed-wheel steps and 153 synthetic benchmark attempts (132 completed, 21 partial). Sixteen Storybook cases carry forward for unchanged frontend bytes; 24 app captures were reviewed. This is bounded local verification, not full accessibility or production certification. The LAB seccomp profile adds a reviewed chroot rule so Chromium sandbox initialization works with all container capabilities dropped. The prerelease label is v0.1.0-prototype. The public source package excludes private approval history, raw evidence stores and runtime screenshots.
