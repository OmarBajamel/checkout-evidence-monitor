# Historical research rationale and synthetic evaluation

The measured results below belong to v0.1.0 on September 24, 2026. The current alpha adds a [prespecified evaluation protocol](EVALUATION_V1_1.md); no new participant or experiment outcome is claimed.

The implementation question is whether configured journey context helps reviewers explain observed checkout differences without overstating visibility. It is not a claim of new malware detection, complete compliance automation or universal store support. Browser-measurement research informed the distinction between a document, a reference, a request, a response and execution.

The protocol defines HTTP-only, PAGE and JOURNEY arms on fixed synthetic fixture version1, identical resource budgets and fresh contexts. The completed run used three repetitions for applicable scenarios, rotates arm order by repetition and records a deterministic ordering seed. T01–T15 and T19 cover principal observations; T16–T18 and T20–T24 are correctness/safety cases. T25–T38 are separate API/UI/publication/recovery cases. There is no implied216-successful-run denominator.

Ground truth comes from fixture source and declared scenario files, not collector output. The inventory metric collapses unique fixture basenames while raw evidence retains frame/state identities; this simplification is explicit and valid only for these fixtures. Full-scenario coverage and arm-capability recall are separate. Change labels have their own applicability. Field completeness, time, retained events and artifact size remain visible. A denominator of zero is null/N/A, never100%. Failed/partial/cancelled attempts stay in reliability totals. Keyword screening for unsupported conclusions is only a review aid; manual conclusion review remains required.

The local experiment executed153 attempts on2026-09-24:144 candidates and 9 baselines, with 132 COMPLETED and 21 PARTIAL observations retained. TESTING_STATUS.md summarizes source provenance, measured counts and limits. The private report retains source/dependency/browser/image/fixture hashes, original failures and fixes, and a complete artifact-integrity/metric audit. An agent reviewed emitted conclusion templates and representative evidence contexts separately from keyword screening. Nonzero journey benefit is not an acceptance requirement. A mixed or negative result is valid. No broad statistical generalization follows from a small purposive fixture set.

Relevant primary technical references include [Playwright network documentation](https://playwright.dev/python/docs/network), [CDP Network domain](https://chromedevtools.github.io/devtools-protocol/tot/Network/), [OWASP ASVS 5.0.0](https://github.com/OWASP/ASVS/tree/v5.0.0), [OpenWPM](https://github.com/openwpm/OpenWPM), and [PCI SSC payment-page guidance](https://blog.pcisecuritystandards.org/new-information-supplement-payment-page-security-and-preventing-e-skimming). These sources inform bounded design choices and do not endorse the prototype.
